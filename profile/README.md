# GeoAI Alignment, Inc.

**Geospatial intelligence and AI agents for research and decision support.**

GeoAI Alignment, Inc. ((주)지오에이아이얼라인먼트) is an independent company
established in 2025 and based in Daegu, Republic of Korea. We develop GeoAI
and AI-agent technologies that connect geospatial data with scientific
analysis, forecasting, and simulation tools.

[Website](https://geoaialign.ai/) ·

[Contact](mailto:contact@geoaialign.com)

## Research and development

- **Environmental modelling:** groundwater, subsurface processes, and water-resource analysis.
- **Geospatial AI:** satellite-derived information and spatial data for environmental and hazard studies.
- **Scientific agents:** tool-connected workflows for simulation, forecasting, and evidence-based decision support.

## Selected public repositories

| Repository | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| [GeoAI THM Studio](https://github.com/geoaialignment/geoai-thm-studio) | Agent-assisted OpenGeoSys modelling of subsurface heat, flow, deformation, and transport. |
